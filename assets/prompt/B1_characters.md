```text
Create a 2.5D isometric game character sprite sheet (5 characters) on a solid flat pure white background with wide empty spacing between each character, no overlapping, no ground tiles, no text or labels.

Strict 2.5D Isometric Camera & Style (Minimal Hand-Painted Isometric):
- True 2.5D isometric game projection (standard 2:1 isometric dimetric camera angle, elevated ~30 degrees, 45-degree diagonal orientation so characters stand on an isometric plane facing front-right-down).
- Gameplay-first low detail: clean readable silhouettes, simple shapes, minimal surface noise.
- Soft painterly cel shading, top-left 45-degree warm daylight, crisp uniform dark indigo-slate ink outline (#1E293B).
- Unified color palette:
  - Soft Jade Green (#6BBF9B) & Deep Jade Moss (#4E9A7A)
  - Warm Cream (#F5EFE0) & Carved Stone (#E4D8BE)
  - Sky Cyan (#7CC6EE)
  - Muted Crimson (#C95B5B) & Deep Crimson (#8F3439)
  - Warm Gold (#E8BE54)
  - Deep Indigo-Slate (#1E293B)

Layout (5 separate characters in a clean 3x2 grid, consistent relative scale):
1. Top-left (1.0x scale, Hero): Cute chibi Chinese monkey warrior, muted crimson martial arts outfit (#C95B5B), warm gold headband and waist sash (#E8BE54), warm cream highlights (#F5EFE0), holding a simple wooden staff.
2. Top-center (0.75x scale, Ranged Enemy): Cute round cloud slime creature, soft jade green body (#6BBF9B, #4E9A7A shading) with fluffy warm cream cloud wisps (#F5EFE0) on top.
3. Top-right (0.9x scale, Melee Enemy): Cute chibi horned imp, muted crimson skin (#C95B5B, #8F3439 shading), dark horns (#1E293B), holding a simple wooden club.

5. Bottom-right (2.75x scale, Boss): Giant imposing stone guardian boss, carved stone armor (#E4D8BE, #F5EFE0), jade moss crown (#4E9A7A), bright glowing sky cyan runes (#7CC6EE), warm gold trim (#E8BE54).

Negative constraints: No flat 2D side-view, no pure 90-degree top-down view, no photorealism, no cast shadows on background, no overlapping sprites, no text.
```
