# Batch 4 — Interactive Props (`B4_props`)

Tạo 1 ảnh sheet rồi thả vào `assets/inbox/B4_props.png`.

```text
Create a 2.5D isometric game interactive props sheet (5 props) on a solid flat pure white background with wide empty spacing between each prop, no overlapping, no ground floor, no text or labels.

Strict 2.5D Isometric Camera & Style (Minimal Hand-Painted Isometric):
- True 2.5D isometric game projection (standard 2:1 isometric dimetric camera angle, elevated ~30 degrees, 45-degree diagonal orientation showing clear top and two isometric side faces).
- Gameplay-first low detail: strong readable silhouettes, clear interactive focal points, minimal surface clutter.
- Soft painterly cel shading, top-left 45-degree warm daylight, crisp dark indigo-slate ink outline (#1E293B).
- Unified color palette:
  - Warm Cream (#F5EFE0) & Carved Stone (#E4D8BE)
  - Warm Gold (#E8BE54) & Deep Gold (#C4932B)
  - Sky Cyan (#7CC6EE)
  - Soft Jade Green (#6BBF9B) & Deep Jade Moss (#4E9A7A)
  - Deep Crimson (#8F3439)
  - Deep Indigo-Slate (#1E293B)

Layout (5 separate isometric props in a clean 3x2 grid):
1. Top-left (Gate of Heaven, large): 2.5D isometric Chinese celestial archway gate aligned to isometric diagonal, warm cream pillars (#F5EFE0), warm gold roof trim (#E8BE54, #C4932B), subtle glowing sky cyan portal energy (#7CC6EE) in center.
2. Top-center (Rune Pillar, tall): 2.5D isometric carved stone pillar (#E4D8BE) showing top and two front faces, glowing sky cyan runes (#7CC6EE), jade moss around base (#4E9A7A).
3. Top-right (Reward Chest): 2.5D isometric treasure chest aligned to isometric diagonal, deep crimson-wood body (#8F3439), warm gold metal bands (#E8BE54), warm cream highlight (#F5EFE0).
4. Bottom-left (Pickup Orb, small): 2.5D isometric glowing collectible orb, warm gold core (#E8BE54) with cream highlight (#F5EFE0) and soft sky cyan aura (#7CC6EE).
5. Bottom-right (Stone Lantern): 2.5D isometric stone garden lantern, carved stone (#E4D8BE), warm gold inner glow (#E8BE54), touch of jade moss (#6BBF9B) on base.

Negative constraints: No flat 2D side-view, no pure 90-degree top-down view, no photorealism, no cast shadows on background, no overlapping props, no text.
```
